EU Job Lens privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

EU Job Lens is a browser extension that shows an estimated monthly net salary and key facts (German language requirement, visa sponsorship, work mode, relocation help) on job ads.

What the extension reads

On www.linkedin.com, www.stepstone.de and de.indeed.com, the extension reads the job ad you have open: the salary, the location and the description text. It uses this only to calculate the estimate and find the facts shown on that page, and keeps none of it after you leave the page. It does not read other sites, other tabs or anything you type.

What the extension stores

Your tax settings (tax class, federal state, number of children, church tax) are saved in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.sync). If you have turned on Chrome sync, Google syncs these settings across your own browsers, as it does for other extensions. Nothing else is stored.

What the extension sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. It has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
